# Job Fit Criteria — Igor Pivnyk

## Language

- The job description must be written in English. Skip non-English postings.
- Exception: Israeli roles (see below) may be in Hebrew.

## Work arrangement

- Accept only when the posting **explicitly** states fully remote work, OR
  offers relocation with visa sponsorship/support. Skip if it is silent on
  remote, relocation, AND sponsorship.
- Either signal is sufficient — do not require both remote and relocation.
- Skip a remote role that is restricted to a specific country or region Igor
  cannot work from (e.g. "Remote — United Kingdom", "US only", "Europe only",
  "must be authorized to work in the US", "W2 only"), UNLESS it offers
  relocation/visa sponsorship. Worldwide/anywhere remote — and roles that
  include Israel, the Middle East, or EMEA — are acceptable. Note: aggregators
  often carry this restriction in the location field, not the description.

## Employment type

- Full-time preferred; also accept contract, freelance, and part-time IF fully
  remote.
- Skip contract/freelance/part-time roles that are on-site or hybrid, unless the
  job is in Israel.

## Seniority

- Senior preferred. If seniority is unstated, assume senior and treat as a match.
- Skip explicitly junior or entry-level roles.

## Technology stack

- Must be iOS/macOS focused (Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, Objective-C, Objective-C++,
  AppKit).
- Skip if the MAIN technology is a cross-platform framework (React Native,
  Flutter, Xamarin, Ionic, Kotlin Multiplatform). A secondary/optional
  cross-platform mention is fine.

## Industry

- Skip crypto/Web3 companies. No other industry restrictions.

## Salary

- No salary filter — notify regardless of stated compensation.

## Israeli jobs

Igor is based in Israel, so Israeli roles are exempt from the Language and
Work-arrangement rules above: no English requirement, and no relocation/visa
sponsorship needed.

- Accept all employment types (full-time, part-time, contract, freelance).
- Accept fully remote Israeli roles.
- Accept hybrid or on-site Israeli roles UNLESS the posting explicitly requires
  4 or more days per week in the office (e.g. "4 days/week in office", "fully
  on-site"). If it is silent on attendance, says only "hybrid", or requires 3 or
  fewer days, treat it as acceptable.
- Crypto/Web3 exclusion and all other filters still apply.

## Timezone

- Igor is based in Israel (UTC+3). If a role requires strict US-only working
  hours, do NOT skip — include a note flagging the timezone mismatch so he can
  decide.
